#**TRK-APS**

##**Warehouse & Inventory Management System**

TRK-APS is a warehouse and inventory management system designed to streamline the planning, procurement, receiving, requisition, picking, and issuance of materials.

The system provides an end-to-end workflow for managing material requirements and inventory movements, helping organizations maintain accurate inventory records, improve material traceability, and standardize warehouse operations.

##**Overview**

TRK-APS manages the complete lifecycle of materials—from forecasting future requirements and initiating procurement requests to receiving purchased items and issuing materials to requesting departments.

The system is organized around the following core transactions:  
• Material Forecast – Plan and forecast future material requirements.  
• Purchase Request – Create and manage requests for materials that need to be procured.  
• Purchase Order – Process approved purchase requests into supplier orders.  
• Receiving – Record and validate materials received from suppliers.  
• Material Requisition – Request materials from available warehouse inventory.  
• Picklist – Prepare and organize requested materials for warehouse picking.  
• Issuance – Record the release of materials from the warehouse to the requesting department or user.  

##**Objectives**

TRK-APS aims to provide a centralized platform that helps organizations:  
• Improve inventory accuracy  
• Standardize warehouse processes  
• Reduce manual inventory transactions  
• Improve material traceability  
• Streamline procurement and receiving workflows  
• Provide better visibility of material movement  
• Support efficient warehouse operations

• Maintain a reliable history of inventory transactions
